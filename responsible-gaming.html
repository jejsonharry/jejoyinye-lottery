Responsible Gaming

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Lottery Is Entertainment, Not Income

Every lottery involves risk and an uncertain outcome. There is no strategy or prediction that can guarantee a win.

1. Adults Only

You must be at least 18 years old to participate. Agents and adults should not place bets for minors or allow minors to access gaming accounts or terminals.

2. Set a Firm Budget

  • Decide your spending limit before you start.
  • Use only disposable money—not rent, food, school fees, business funds or borrowed money.
  • Do not increase your stake to recover a previous loss.
  • Keep track of both time and money spent.

3. Know the Warning Signs

Take action if gaming is causing secrecy, borrowing, arguments, missed responsibilities, anxiety, repeated attempts to recover losses, or difficulty stopping.

4. Take Back Control

  • Stop and take a cooling-off break.
  • Remove quick access to gaming apps and payment methods.
  • Ask the relevant platform about limits, account restriction or self-exclusion tools.
  • Speak with a trusted family member, counsellor or qualified health professional.

5. Protect Your Account

Never share your password, PIN or one-time code. Do not let another person use your identity or bank details to create a gaming account.

6. Our Commitment

Jejoyinye Lottery Services does not encourage underage play, borrowing to gamble, chasing losses or claims of guaranteed winnings. Promotional and prediction content should always be understood in that context.

Need Support?

Pause gaming immediately and contact the relevant gaming platform to request account controls. You may also contact us through the Contact page for help locating the appropriate platform support channel.
